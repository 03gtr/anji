# The final story — «الباقي إلنا»

The owner's final brief: fix the board, finish Ryan and Anji's story in at most four chapters plus the choice, give two real endings, and publish. **This is the last major story update.**

## Shape

- **Four chapters, no more.** They follow Season 3's finale and live in Season 3's own document.
- **No new place, map, puzzle or system.** It reuses:
  - the places: her room, the club, the lane at dawn, her street at night;
  - the cast, Zefiro, and the V8 sounds;
  - the existing dialogue, cinematic and choice tools.

## Chapter 01 — Marius is not blind

- Marius has noticed and asks plainly: «ملاحظ ريان كلش مقرّب منچ بالفترة الأخيرة… معجب بيچ لو شنو؟». The owner's line is written in the game's spelling (چ).
- She blushes and answers (`marius-ask`):
  - **deny:** «ريان صديقك، مو صديقي», then changes the subject;
  - **joke:** «معجب بزفيرو. وبس.»;
  - **ask back:** «ليش تسأل؟».
- He reads her face, not her words: «إي… واضح أكو شي، بس خليني ساكت. مو كلشي لازم أسأل عنه.»
- Her phone: Ryan passed his driving test (Season 3's medical report was for it) and wants her to be the first to know.

## Chapter 02 — Ryan has limits

**The effort.** At the club Ryan shows his licence and a card he made: «أول راكبة: أنجي». Sabri teases him in front of everyone, and she answers (`club-moment`):
- **deny:** «ورقة نادي»;
- **own:** «إي… بس لا تكتب بالكروب»;
- **quiet:** says nothing.

**How far his anger goes is earned, not forced:**
- **The sharp line** «يا معفنة، مو كل مرة أتحمل وأسكت!» comes only when she brushes him off in public **and** has denied him before (to Marius in 01, or to Sabri in Season 3's Mission 01). He names those denials first.
- **A public brush-off with no history:** he raises his voice, without the insult.
- **She owns it, or stays silent:** he is hurt and says so quietly.
- **No violence**, and nothing romantic about the anger.

**She answers him (`after-words`), and that decides his way back:**
- **She stands up for herself** («لا تحچي ويايا هيچ»): he apologises for the word itself, specifically, and keeps his legitimate boundary: «ما أگدر أبقى الشي اللي تنكرينه كل ما أحد سأل. هذا حدّي.»
- **She asks why:** he explains the hurt and why, and apologises for the voice and the word.
- **She goes silent:** he asks for time, «زعلان من نفسي هم», and apologises.

**Both own their part.**
- Her fear of the rumour (the whole of Season 3) and her denials: «أنكرتك هواي. وهذا مو عدل عليك.»
- His voice, the word, and not seeing how hard it was for her.

**Forgiveness is hers to time (`forgive`):** «خلص… بس لا تعيدها» or «أحتاج وقت». He accepts either.

**Marius** arrives, reads the faces, and keeps quiet a second time.

## Chapter 03 — The things we never said

A quiet walk at dawn with Ryan and Snooky. There is nothing to fail. She stops where she likes, in any order:
- **The grate — fear:** hers of the talk; his of being «صديق أخوية ماريوس» for ever, of her being ashamed.
- **The tyre marks — expectations:** that she stand by him in front of the people who matter; that he be patient. Neither said so.
- **The oil stain — respect (`need`):** to be seen, not the rumours; to be told when he is hurt; time to choose by herself. His need: not to be a secret to the people who matter to her.

Ryan asks «إحنا… جاهزين نبني شي؟». She names the place she will answer: tonight, under the trees by her home. Marius texts her: he is covering for her, and «لا تسأليني شلون عرفت».

## Chapter 04 — The final choice

- Zefiro idles under the trees where it once spoke for him.
- Ryan speaks for himself:
  - his feelings, from the first blue rose to this street;
  - the future he hopes for: an ordinary morning, Snooky ruining everything, and asking for her hand «رسمي… گدام كل اللي يهمنا»;
  - only if she wants it.
- He reads her `need` back to her and does not press: «جاوبي علمودچ».
- **No ceremony, no date, no ring, no family.** Earlier owner rules keep her family out of the story; the choice is about moving toward marriage, and nothing more.

### Ending A — «إي. أختارك.»

- She chooses, in her own words: «لأني أريد».
- He laughs and wipes his eyes at once.
- A quiet embrace under the trees, and Zefiro's lights blink twice, as on the first night.
- Marius comes out, sees, and finally says what he guessed all week, half laughing and half proud: «آني مو أعمى… تعال باچر للبيت. رسمي… وأهلاً بيك. صدگ.» His line reads her earlier words.
- Card: «النهاية — الباقي إلنا».

### Ending B — «مو هسه.»

- A real ending, not a punishment. She is not cruel: «مو لأن ما أحبك… أريد أختار يوم ما أخاف».
- He is not diminished: «ما أريدچ تختاريني وإنتِ خايفة… أحترم جوابچ».
- He will not ask again unless she opens the door.
- He drives away slowly, and the silence carries it.
- Marius does not ask. He stands with her: «مو لازم تحچين… تعالي، أسويلچ چاي.»
- Card: «النهاية — مو هسه».

### «الاختيار الأخير، مرة ثانية» — replaying the choice

- **From the journal:** the entry «الاختيار الأخير» has a button. It asks first and states that the first choice stays recorded.
- **On her street:** a spot under the trees offers the same, with the same question.
- **What a replay does:** goes back to the moment of choice without restarting anything. The chapters and the seasons stay done, and Zefiro and Ryan are back if ending B had sent them away.
- **The record (`end3:` effect, Season 3's document):**
  - `s3:end:first` — the first ending, kept for ever and never overwritten;
  - `s3:end:last` — the latest choice;
  - `s3:end:history` — every choice, in order (`yes,later,yes`…);
  - `s3:dec:final` — the latest answer, which the scenes read.
- A full restart of the game keeps the old documents aside (`:previous`, the existing `all` policy). The first ending of a finished playthrough is never deleted.
